# Nephele icon concepts

**Recommendation: concept 1, Golden guardian.** Its open horn crown and tapered face form the most confident silhouette of the set. It connects directly to the rescuing golden ram, holds together at 16–32 px, and gives Nephele an identity beyond a generic cloud utility. The tiny face highlight is secondary; recognition comes from the outer shape.

## Construction and remaining refinement

Hand-authored paths, no external fonts or imagery, no filters. All artboards are 1024 × 1024. Tiles have a 32-unit inset and a 215-unit corner radius (22.4% of the 960-unit tile). Shared warm palette: #F6821F, #FBAD41, #15110F, #2E2824 and #F2EFEA, with restrained amber and ivory highlights. The tile edge and directional gradients provide the depth; the marks do not depend on shadows.

Before committing to the lead, refine the horn openings and muzzle taper in the actual Dock. A dedicated 16 px optical variant should omit the highlight and slightly widen the horn gaps. Check a flat one-colour export as well as the coloured tile. The current SVGs are concept masters, not a packaged app-icon asset catalogue.

The other directions need more work before selection: soften the lightning reading in 2, make the fleece terminal more explicit in 3, simplify the internal turn in 4, and enlarge the face/neck negative space in 5. Choose one direction before investing in those refinements.

## Similarity considerations

These are original drawings, but originality of execution does not establish exclusive rights. From visual judgment only: concept 1 shares the broad ram-head/Aries category with Ram Trucks and numerous sports identities; its open crown and long central taper need particular comparison. Concept 2 occupies a crowded cloud/lightning category. Concept 3 competes with many N monograms. Concept 4 can suggest a G, wool badge or directional arrow. Concept 5 belongs to the classical cameo/profile tradition and may evoke established profile-based brands such as Wella.

No external similarity search was performed, in accordance with the offline brief. This is not trademark clearance. Check the selected silhouette and name before public release.
